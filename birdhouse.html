How to start birding

Never seen a bird in your life? I can help! Here are some common backyard birds you might spot in the Northeast.

American Robin

One of my favorites, these little guys like to do their signature little hops. Extremely cute!

Northern Cardinal

They can be quite red.

Blue Jay

Very obnoxious beauty. Their blue feathers are not essentially blue, but a result of structural coloration that manipulates light to appear blue.
